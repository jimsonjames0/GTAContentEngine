# GTA VI Content Engine — Editorial Style Guide

**Version:** 1.0 (Month 2)  
**Purpose:** Define the channel’s editorial identity and enforce quality rules across all script generation.

---

## Channel Identity

### Target Audience
Core GTA and Rockstar Games enthusiasts who want sharp, perceptive analysis of Vice City's mechanics, world design, and verified launch milestones.

### Tone
Knowledgeable insider, observant, concise, grounded, slightly dry wit. Zero hype-screaming or artificial clickbait.

### Differentiation
Connects small visual details or store milestones to how Rockstar actually engineers open worlds, strictly separating confirmed facts from fan theories.

---

## Narrative Rules

### Hook
Must open with a sharp observation, contrast, or specific anomaly in the first 3 seconds without repeating the raw Reddit post title verbatim.

### Body
Must include at least one concrete mechanic, history parallel (e.g., RDR2 or GTA V), or specific visual detail. Must label community theories explicitly.

### Payoff
Must conclude with a concrete takeaway answering why the detail matters to the player experience.

---

## Banned Phrases

The following phrases are strictly prohibited in all generated scripts:

1. `Details like this demonstrate the level of density`
2. `pushing next-gen immersion further`
3. `Drop your thoughts below and stay tuned`
4. `I just found something interesting:`
5. `Rockstar hid this in plain sight:`
6. `Look at this GTA VI detail:`
7. `Nobody noticed this:`
8. `Take a closer look at`
9. `trailer..` (double-period punctuation artifact)
10. `This shifts how fans are viewing the current timeline`

---

## Rejected Content Types

The following content types must never appear in published videos:

1. Unverified 4chan leaks presented as fact
2. Clickbait release date countdowns
3. Verbatim reading of Reddit user comments

---

## Source Attribution Rules

- Reddit threads are used for topic discovery only. They are never treated as official Rockstar confirmation unless explicitly linked to an official Rockstar announcement.
- Community theories must be labeled as theories, never presented as confirmed facts.
- Never reproduce Reddit user comments verbatim in scripts.
