GTA VI CHANNEL MANIFESTO

1. WHO THIS CHANNEL IS FOR

People who are excited about GTA VI and want entertaining,
interesting, and easy-to-digest content about the game.

The audience should feel like they are getting something
worth watching even if the video is only 30 seconds long.

2. WHAT THIS CHANNEL PROMISES

Every video should provide at least one of:

- Something interesting
- Something surprising
- Something funny
- Something useful
- Something worth discussing

The channel should feel:

- Fast
- Entertaining
- Authentic
- Curious
- Knowledgeable
- Internet-native

The goal is to make viewers think:

"I didn't know that."

"I never noticed that."

"That's actually interesting."

or

"That was funny."

3. CONTENT PILLARS

Primary:

- GTA VI discoveries
- GTA VI news
- GTA VI theories
- GTA VI analysis
- GTA VI humor
- GTA VI gameplay

Secondary:

- GTA lore
- GTA V comparisons
- Rockstar Games history
- Community discussions
- Interesting details surrounding GTA VI

4. WHAT WE WILL NEVER POST

- Lazy reposts
- Stolen creator content
- Misleading information presented as fact
- Fake news
- Fake leaks presented as real
- Low-effort AI-generated spam
- Videos created only to hit a posting quota
- Misleading clickbait
- Content we would not personally watch

5. ORIGINALITY RULE

We can use existing footage and information as source material,
but the finished content should add meaningful original value.

This can include:

- Commentary
- Analysis
- Context
- Storytelling
- Humor
- Explanation
- Original editing
- Original narration

6. QUALITY RULE

We would rather publish one excellent video than four mediocre
videos.

Automation exists to make good content faster.

It does not exist to make bad content faster.

7. AI RULE

AI is an assistant, not the creator.

AI may help with:

- Research
- Ideas
- Scripts
- Voice
- Editing
- Captions
- Organization

But the final content must still feel intentional and human.

8. SUCCESS DEFINITION

Month 1 success is NOT:

- Followers
- Viral videos
- Revenue
- Millions of views

Month 1 success is:

Build a repeatable system capable of producing good GTA VI
content efficiently.

FIRST GOAL:

Create ONE genuinely good video from the pipeline.
